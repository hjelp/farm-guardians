Farm Guardians

Hungry pests are marching on your farm, and only you can stop them.

A relaxed but surprisingly deep tower defense game with a warm, storybook farm look. Plant towers along the country roads, upgrade them, and keep your crops safe from rabbits, crows, moles, beetles, boars and worse.

Coming soon to the App Store

What's in the game

  • 8 farm towers: from the trusty Corn Cannon to the Solar Lens, each with 3 levels and 2 specializations.
  • Farmer Joe: a hero who fights on the road beside your towers, levels up and learns new abilities.
  • 12 campaign maps and 4 boss fights: across orchards, riversides, autumn hills and frosty valleys.
  • Endless Frontier lands that never run out, plus a new Daily Challenge every day.
  • Things to work towards: research upgrades, a Farmer Ladder, daily quests, achievements and a Wheel of Fortune.
  • Casual, Normal and Veteran difficulty, and challenge modes with special rules.
  • Plays offline. No account, no ads and no tracking.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?
Open Settings (the gear), go to Progress and choose Reset all progress. Tap it twice to confirm.

A map is too hard. What can I do?
Try Casual difficulty, buy research upgrades on the Upgrades screen, use items like the Frost Jar, or replay earlier maps for gold and XP. You keep everything you earn, even when you lose.

The game pauses when I leave it.
That's on purpose: a call or switching apps pauses the match, so you never lose a run by accident.
