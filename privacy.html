Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Farm Guardians ("the game").

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the game stores

Your progress and settings, such as unlocked maps, stars, upgrades and sound preferences, are saved only on your device. We never receive this data. Deleting the game deletes it. You can also erase it at any time under Settings → Progress → Reset all progress.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics services.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not require an account or an internet connection.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone or photo library. iOS may list these permissions because they are part of the game engine, but the game never asks for or uses them.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as optional anonymous gameplay statistics or advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Any such feature will ask for your permission first where the law or the App Store requires it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
