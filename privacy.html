Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Farm Guardians ("the game").

The short version

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. If you agree when the game asks, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics that help us make it better. If you say no, nothing leaves your device. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy.

What the game stores

Your progress and settings, such as unlocked maps, stars, upgrades and sound preferences, are saved only on your device. We never receive this data. Deleting the game deletes it. You can also erase it at any time under Settings → Progress → Reset all progress.

Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous statistics. Only if you say yes, the game sends information like this:

  • Which maps, modes and difficulty you play, how far you get, whether you win, and on which wave you lose.
  • Which towers, upgrades, items and features (quests, wheel, shop) you use, and in-game gold and gems earned and spent.
  • How you play: how often you speed up the game or call waves early, how much damage each tower does, and which pests get through.
  • Your game settings (for example sound on or off, chosen music) and when you first reach milestones such as your first win.
  • Session length, the app version, your device model, operating system, language setting and the game's frame rate, so we can fix performance problems.
  • Error reports when something goes wrong in the game: the error message and where in the game's code it happened, with file paths from your device removed.
  • A random ID created by the game when it is installed, so that events from the same installation can be grouped. It is not your Apple ID, advertising ID or any other identifier from your device, and it is not linked to your name or any account.

We use this only to find maps that are too easy or too hard, see which features people enjoy, and fix bugs. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or combine it with data from other apps or websites.

The statistics are processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt) and are deleted after at most 12 months. To stop sending statistics, turn off Share anonymous play data in Settings → Privacy. To have statistics already sent from your device deleted, contact us (see below).

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising services.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not require an account. It works fully offline. Statistics, if you allowed them, are sent later when you're online.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone or photo library. iOS may list these permissions because they are part of the game engine, but the game never asks for or uses them.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and the anonymous statistics are only sent after the player agrees.

Changes

If a future version handles data in a new way, for example advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the game will ask for your permission first where the law or the App Store requires it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
